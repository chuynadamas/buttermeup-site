Privacy Policy

ButterMeUp! · Last updated 6 August 2026

ButterMeUp! does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind in the app.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress and preferences locally on your device using Apple's standard app storage. This never leaves your device unless you use Apple's own iCloud device backup. It includes:

  • Which levels you have unlocked, and your best number of tries
  • Which breads you have collected in each level
  • Your best distance in Infinity mode
  • Your settings: sound on or off, music track, and tutorial progress

Deleting the app removes all of it.

Game Center

If you choose to sign in to Game Center, your scores and achievements are submitted to Apple's Game Center service so that leaderboards and achievements work. That data is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy, not by us. We never receive your Apple Account, your real name, or any contact details.

Game Center is entirely optional. Declining it does not limit the game: every level, bread and mode remains fully playable, and your scores are still saved on your device.

Children

The game is rated 4+ and is safe for children. Because we collect no data at all, we collect nothing from children either. There is no chat, no user-generated content, no advertising, and no web browsing in the app.

No purchases

ButterMeUp! is a one-time purchase. There are no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, and no payment information is ever handled by the app.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above.
