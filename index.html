ButterMeUp!

A stick of butter runs for its life across a very hot kitchen. Keep moving, keep your shape.

Coming soon to the App Store iPhone and iPad · in review

What it is

An arcade auto-runner with one twist: you are made of butter, and you are melting the whole time. Fire platforms speed you up and cook you twice as fast. Snow slows the melt but turns every landing into a skid. Honey grabs your feet. Jelly launches you. The bar in the corner is your life, and it is always going down.

What is in it

Eleven hand-built levels

A campaign that teaches one idea at a time and then combines them, ending somewhere genuinely hard.

33 hidden breads

Three per level, tucked down risky routes. Dying resets them, so a clean 3 of 3 has to be earned in one run.

Endless mode

Infinity generates fresh ground forever. All-time and weekly leaderboards.

Seventeen achievements

From your first flip to collecting every last bread in the game.

No accounts, no ads, no tracking

Pay once. The game collects nothing and makes no network requests. Progress stays on your device.

Original music and sound

Three lo-fi tracks written for the game, switchable or off. Every sound generated from scratch.
