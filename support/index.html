ButterMeUp!

Support & help · a stick of butter with somewhere to be

How to play

  • Tap to jump. Tap again in the air to double jump.
  • Hold the tap to jump higher.
  • You melt as you run. Collect butter to top yourself up.
  • Land upside down for a speed boost, and chain flips for more.

The surfaces

  • Fire — speeds you up, but melts you about twice as fast.
  • Snow — slows your melt, but you slide.
  • Honey — grips you and takes away your flip.
  • Jelly — launches you high.

Frequently asked

I keep running out of butter.

Melting is the timer. Take the butter you pass, and remember that fire is the fastest way to lose it. On some levels, staying melted (and therefore small and quick) is the intended strategy rather than a mistake.

How do the breads work?

Every level hides three. You have to collect all three in a single clean run, because dying resets them. The level select shows exactly which of the three you already have, so you can see which one you keep missing.

My score did not appear on the leaderboard.

Leaderboards need Game Center. Open the Game Center bubble on the main menu to check you are signed in. If you dismissed the sign-in prompt, you can sign in from the iOS Settings app under Game Center, then relaunch the game.

Can I replay the tutorial?

Yes. Settings → Replay Tutorial.

Can I turn off the music but keep sound effects?

Yes. Settings → Music cycles through the three tracks and Off. The separate Sound toggle controls everything.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes it, so it is worth keeping your device backup on if you care about your records.
